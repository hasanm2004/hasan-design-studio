FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before placing your design order.

How can I place an order? +

You can place your order directly through WhatsApp by clicking any Order Now button on our website.

What payment method do you accept? +

We accept payment through available local payment methods. Payment details will be shared when you place your order.

Do I need to pay in advance? +

Yes. Our payment policy is 50% advance payment and 50% final payment after the design is approved.

How many revisions are included? +

The number of revisions depends on your selected package or service. The revision limit is clearly mentioned with each package.

How long does it take to receive my design? +

Delivery time depends on the type and complexity of the design. The estimated delivery time will be discussed when you place your order.

What are your business hours? +

We are available Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.

Can I request a custom design? +

Yes. If you need a design that is not included in our packages or price list, contact us on WhatsApp and discuss your requirements with us.
